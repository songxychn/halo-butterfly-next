This theme contains adaptations of upstream styles and extracted character tables,
not just a reference build. Halo Butterfly Next contributors changed Stylus to
SCSS, adapted selectors/settings for Halo templates, and changed the character
tables to ES module exports. Existing per-file source and change notes are retained.
The GPL-3.0 license of the overall theme does not replace these upstream notices.
Behavior-only compatibility implementations are not all listed as copied material.
